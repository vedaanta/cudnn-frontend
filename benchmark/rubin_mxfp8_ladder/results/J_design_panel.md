## Ceiling analysis (judge)

Units: util = 544 tensor clocks per 256 Q rows x 128 KV tokens divided by the step; 70 % needs a step <= 777 clk, 80 % <= 680. Calibration note: the task's F anchor (1400 clk, 39 %) is the PIC/model convention; the board's own ncu for F at 32k none gives 2774 us at 2.318 GHz over 19.69 x 256 steps = ~1276 clk/step and tensor-active 43.7 %, so every absolute figure here carries ~+4 points of uncertainty; the bounds below are in clocks, which is what the designs change, and the conclusions do not move.

Bound 1 -- the issue port (the single capping fact). With fp32 S and the f16 exponent the softmax is 4 instructions per 2 elements (FFMA2 scale-shift, F2FP f32x2->f16x2, MUFU.EX2.F16x2, F2FP f16x2x2->fp8) = 256 irreducible + ~40 overhead = 297 per warp per 128 columns (F SASS: 296/302 for WG0/WG1), and D6's ptxas check shows no 4-wide fp8 pack from f16x2 exists on sm_10x (cvt.rs.e4m3x4.f32 needs f32 sources, i.e. f32 exp, which doubles MUFU to 1040 clk/step). Two softmax warps per SMSP therefore need 594 issue slots per 544 tensor clocks -- 109 % of the tensor time before any other warp issues -- plus the MMA/correction/loader/scheduler slots measured at ~90-160 per SMSP-step (ncu issue 53.8 % x step - 594). Cap at a 100 %-busy port: 544/(594+92..160) = 72-79 %. The port never runs at 100 %: a lone burst runs at ~0.6 IPC on dependency stalls and two interleaved warps reach ~85-90 %, giving 760-890 clk = 61-72 %. So 70 % is at the edge of the issue port and 80 % (<= 680 clk, i.e. <= 86 slots for everything that is not softmax, at a perfect port) is unreachable with 297 instructions. Every lever that raises this cap is small: suspend-hint waits on the non-softmax warps (-40..80 slots), HOIST/D6 overhead trims (-10..15 instr/warp), production double-arrive and NOSCALE (-5..8 instr); stacked they move the perfect-port cap to ~86 % and the realistic one to 70-75 %.

Bound 2 -- MUFU: 2 x 65 x 4 = 520 clk per SMSP-step = 96 % of the tensor time. Not binding alone, but it is the co-binding pipe at any util above ~60 % (xu_mufu is already 41.7 % at F's 43.7 % tensor-active) and any extra EX2 (sinks, has_lse recompute, rescale alpha) tips it.

Bound 3 -- the chain, and why fp32 S matters. Removing PV/BMM1/queue from the S-buffer refill (dedicated P + s_free) leaves L = LDTM ~300 + wake/commit ~100-150 and W = 490 (burst 430 + alpha + store). With fp32 S one buffer is 128 TMEM columns; in 576 columns next to O (128) + Sigma (16) + SF (16-24) + Q (32) + P (64) there is room for exactly one S buffer per WG (A4: 528), so the LDTM can only leave the chain through a register prefetch, and 192 registers (128 live fp32 S) allow that only after chunk b's convert (PREF form): ~90-130 of the 300 clk stay exposed, giving a per-WG period of ~620-660 clk -- just under the issue bound. Without the prefetch the period is W + ~330 = ~850 (A4-base ~60 %). Two buffers per WG would need 128 more columns (656+), and 64-column half-buffers cost ~40-60 fixed instructions per sub-step (G: +27 %), which lowers Bound 1 to 66 %. Hence fp32 S fixes the design space at 'one S buffer per WG plus a register prefetch', and whether that prefetch lands without TMEM->RF writeback stalls (PerfSim G: x9) is the difference between ~60 % and ~68 %.

Conclusion: with fp32 S and the measured 297-instruction step the best achievable mainloop MMA util is ~70 % (A4 + a working PREF-form prefetch + suspend-hint waits + the HOIST/double-arrive/D6 trims, at ~90 % issue-port efficiency); the plain A4 build lands at ~60 %, and 80 % is not reachable -- it would require fewer than 4 softmax instructions per 2 elements, which means f16 S (user-forbidden) or an fp8 pack from f32 sources (MUFU-bound). The fact that caps it is the issue port: 594 softmax issue slots per SMSP per 544 tensor clocks, set by fp32 S + f16 exp + fp8 P with no wider pack available.

## Recommendation (judge)

Implement A4 first, as a three-rung ladder on the local board, and nothing else in parallel except the three one-day probes below. Do not build any two-partial-O variant: with fp32 S the user's literal ask (Q in TMEM + two O partials) is 592/576 at M=128 (A1 headline) and 656+/576 once P gets its own slot, and every two-partial layout that does fit (A1-S/A1-R 560, A2 560/568, B2 560) is forced to alias P into S again, which rebuilds F's PV->BMM1->LDTM chain and caps them at 39-56 %. The decoupling the user wants -- two independent softmax->P pipelines that never wait on each other's PV -- is what A4 delivers with ONE O: sharing O/Sigma (tile-order max ratchet, ~40 clk per tile, correction protocol unchanged) frees 144 columns, which pays for Q in TMEM (32), two dedicated P slots (64), per-WG SF slots and stats, and leaves ~48 spare. Tile findings that constrain everything: tile M must drop to 128 rows per CTA to fit a second S/P pipeline or Q in TMEM at all (cost: 2x K/V L2 traffic, ~17 TB/s = ~45 % of peak -- plan the CGA_N=2 K/V multicast as the follow-up, not a prerequisite); tile N per S buffer must stay 128 because every sub-step pays ~40-60 fixed softmax instructions (G measured +27 % at N=64), which caps the issue port at <=66 % (N=64) / 51 % (N=32); N=256 does not fit fp32 S except as B3b's 3-ring, which cannot land its prefetch.

Why A4 over B3b and D7 (all ~60 % in the corrected model): A4's S buffer is released at wait::ld (s_free), so BMM1(k+2) is retired ~290 clk after the LDTM landing -- exactly the chunk-b prefetch point -- and it has 48 spare TMEM columns; B3b's buffer is released only at PV, so its prefetch arrives ~250 clk late and it sits at 576/576; D7 is killed as written (Sigma-share RMW race, SF time-share WAR, MMA order vs skew), has zero slack, and its closest measured analogue (PREF) lost 4 %. D7-core stays the M=256 fallback (1x K/V traffic) only if A4-base shows l2_pct > 60 % or long-scoreboard stalls on k_full/v_full.

Expected ladder (util = 544 / step-clk per 256 rows x 128 tokens): A4-base (exposed LDTM) 55-62 %; A4 + PREF-form both-chunk prefetch into the registers freed by chunk b's convert 62-68 %; A4 + suspend-hint waits on the non-softmax warps + HOIST + production correction double-arrive + D6 trims 65-72 %. 70 % is reachable only with the full stack and a working prefetch; 80 % is not reachable with fp32 S (see ceiling).

Measure FIRST on the board, before any A4 code (each <= 1 day on F, no layout change, each knob added to bench_ladder._env_from_rung's cache-suffix list and the kmod record):
P1 (decisive): LADDER_PREF_TMEMP=1 -- a timing-only hack on the LADDER_PREF arm that stores P with the classic tcgen05.st into the S tail (P0_OFF/P1_OFF) and runs PV/Sigma as today's TS MMAs instead of the SMEM P slots, keeping s_free early BMM1 and the both-chunk prefetch. Numerics are wrong (BMM1(k+1) clobbers the tail) but S, max, alpha and every barrier are real, so the step time is that of the A4/D7 steady state with P in TMEM and no SMEM-port load. If 32k none lands at <= ~900 clk/step (ncu tensor-active >= 58-60 %) the mechanism is real: build A4. If it stays >= ~1200 clk the step is bound by the two warps' dependency/lockstep behaviour, not the chain, and the whole A4/B3/D7 family is capped at ~F+10 %: pivot to the phase-offset (C4 with two barrier ids) and issue-slot levers before touching the layout.
P2: issue-slot reclaim -- flip the correction/MMA/loader/scheduler ring waits (not the softmax ones) from SPIN_RING_WAITS spin to the suspend-hint form and read the issue-active delta and step time; this sizes the ~90-160 non-softmax slots per SMSP-step (ncu: 0.538 x step - 594) that set A4's issue cap (70-77 % -> up to ~82 % if ~60 slots come back).
P3: free-lever additivity -- F with LADDER_HOIST=1 LADDER_CORR_NORESCALE=2 LADDER_NOSCALE=1 LADDER_SUB2=1 interleaved vs F (expect ~-3..-4.4 %); HOIST and the double-arrive port into A4's body unchanged, NOSCALE is a contract change (scale into the caller's MXFP8 Q quantiser) and is only informational.
Also record on P1/A4-base: ncu l2_pct, tma_ld_mb, stall_long_sb and the spill count (STL/LDL) of the softmax body from the plan object's cubin -- A4's prefetch register peak is ~187-203 of 192 and PREF's spill status was never recorded.
